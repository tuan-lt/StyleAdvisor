# 👔 Style Advisor — 2-Minute Pitch Script (Simple English)

> **Mục tiêu:** Tiếng Anh cực kỳ đơn giản, từ vựng quen thuộc (Basic English), câu ngắn, dễ phát âm và dễ nhớ khi đứng thuyết trình.  
> **Thời gian:** Khoảng 2 phút (nói chậm rãi, tự tin).

---

## 🎙️ Spoken Script (Simple English — Dễ đọc, dễ nhớ)

### [0:00 – 0:20] 1. The Problem (Vấn đề)
"Have you ever looked at your full closet and said: *'I have nothing to wear'*?

We all have this problem before a big day—like a job interview, a wedding, or meeting an investor. We are scared to dress wrong. If you wear a suit, you look too serious. If you wear a hoodie, people might not take you seriously."

### [0:20 – 0:40] 2. What We Learned (Bài học từ người dùng)
"We talked to many young founders and office workers. They told us two simple things:

First: They don't want to look at 50 different outfits. They just want **one good outfit** and a clear reason *why* it works.

Second: They hate asking ChatGPT, because it shows fake clothes with broken links that you cannot buy."

### [0:40 – 0:55] 3. Why We Care (Tại sao quan tâm)
"We know this feeling because we had it too. When you wear the right clothes, you feel confident right away. 

We believe everyone should feel this good, without paying 200 dollars an hour for a private stylist."

### [0:55 – 1:20] 4. Our Solution (Giải pháp & Thiết kế)
"So, we built **Style Advisor**—an AI stylist right on your phone.

It is very easy to use: no login, no long forms. Our main rule is simple: *the AI picks the style, but every single item is 100% real, in stock, and from trusted local stores.*"

### [1:20 – 1:40] 5. What You Can Try Today (Trải nghiệm ngay bây giờ)
"Right now, in less than one minute, you pick your body type, your budget, and where you want to go.

The app gives you one complete outfit immediately. If you think it is *'too fancy'* or *'too casual'*, just click one button and it changes right away. If you already have a white shirt at home, you check the box, and you only buy what you need."

### [1:40 – 2:00] 6. Value, Money & First Users (Giá trị & Cách phát triển)
"Style Advisor saves you time and takes away your stress.

How do we make money? When users click and buy missing items from stores, we get a small fee from the shop.

To get our first users, we are sharing the app directly with startup groups, job-seeker clubs, and local meetups where people want to look great today.

Thank you so much!"
